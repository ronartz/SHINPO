3. Permissions & Privacy

SHINPO Browser Companion is engineered around local privacy and zero data exfiltration.

  • ✓
    Native Messaging Communicates solely with the per-user SHINPO host application installed on this machine over standard input/output.
  • ✓
    Tabs & Web Navigation Evaluates top-level addresses strictly on your device against your active task rules. Browsing history is never collected, logged to disk, or transmitted over the network.
  • ✓
    Page Scripting Injects SHINPO's intervention screen only when an unapproved website is visited during active Focus. It does not read webpage text, credentials, or cookies.
  • ✓
    Local Storage & Alarms Stores the pairing token and local lease snapshot. Automatically expires policy restrictions when your Focus lease elapses.
Zero telemetry guarantee: No remote servers, analytics platforms, or external tracking scripts exist in this extension. All evaluation is local.
